Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Doorlight has one purpose: helping cats and dogs from verified rescues find good homes. We only collect the information we need for that: to sign you in securely and confirm you’re 18 or over, to verify that rescues and shelters are genuine, to share your application with the rescue you choose, and to keep adopters, rescues and animals safe. We don’t sell your data, we don’t use it for advertising, and we only share it with the rescue you apply to and the services that help us run Doorlight. If we ever want to use your data for anything new, we’ll tell you before it happens and ask for your permission where the law requires it.

1. Who we are

Doorlight is currently operated by Maan Hamade, an individual based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, who decides how personal data is used (the controller). Contact: doorlightpets@gmail.com. When Doorlight’s operating company is formed, we’ll update this section and tell you in the app.

2. What we collect in the app

  • Account: your email address and sign-in records. We sign you in with one-time codes sent by email, so we never hold a password.
  • Age and terms: your confirmation that you’re 18 or older, and the date and version of the terms and this policy you accepted.
  • Adopter profile: your home type, whether you have private outdoor space, other pets at home, whether children live with you (an age band only, never names), and your experience with pets.
  • Applications and messages: what you send to rescues about an animal, and their replies, including any meet-and-greet time you choose.
  • Rescue accounts: organisation details, the names and work emails of staff who use the app, the organisation’s location, and the licence or registration documents used to verify it.
  • Listings: animal details and photos posted by rescues.
  • Safety records: reports, blocks and our moderation decisions.
  • Location: if you allow it, your phone’s approximate location is used to sort animals by distance. It’s sent with each search and not stored. If you don’t allow it, we use Dubai.
  • Notifications: a push notification token, only if you turn notifications on.

We don’t collect payment details, because nothing is sold in Doorlight. The app doesn’t use advertising identifiers, analytics tools or crash-reporting tools. If we add any, we’ll update this policy first.

3. This website

doorlightpets.com doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and its fonts are served from this site rather than from a third party. Our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability. If you email us, we use your message only to reply. The account deletion page uses the same sign-in service as the app.

5. Who we share it with

  • The rescue you apply to receives your adopter profile, your note and your messages for that application only.
  • Service providers who run Doorlight for us under contract: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany); Google (sending sign-in emails, and screening uploaded photos for unsafe content); Expo, Apple and Google (delivering push notifications); and our website host.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We don’t sell personal data, and we never share it with sponsors or advertisers.

6. Where your data is processed

Your data is stored in Germany, in the European Union. Some providers above also process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UAE, we rely on the safeguards the law requires, including the providers’ contractual data protection terms.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy in a portable format. You can withdraw consent at any time. To delete your account, go to Profile in the app, use doorlightpets.com/delete-account, or email us. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the UAE’s data protection authority or, if you live in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Doorlight is for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Database rules make sure each person and each rescue can see only what they’re allowed to see, and access by our team is limited to what moderation and support need. If a breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we’ll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
