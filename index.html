Every animal comes from a verified rescue.

No breeders. No sellers. No anonymous posts. Just rescues and shelters we’ve checked, and animals waiting for a home.

Our vision

A loving home for every rescued cat and dog in the UAE.

Our mission

To connect adopters with verified rescues in one free, trusted app, with honest listings, careful matches and no animal ever sold.

What we promise

Verified rescues only

Licensed shelters, and community rescuers vetted through vet references, a track record of rehoming and a signed code of conduct.

Adoption, never sales

No prices and no listings for sale. Selling animals without a licence is illegal in the UAE, and it has no place here.

Honest listings

Every rescue confirms each animal’s vaccinations, microchip and neutering before it goes live.

Free for adopters and rescues

Browsing, applying and listing cost nothing.

How it will work

  1. Browse

    See cats and dogs from verified rescues near you.

  2. Apply once

    Fill in your home profile one time and use it for every application.

  3. Meet and bring them home

    Chat with the rescue, book a meet-and-greet, and welcome your new companion.
