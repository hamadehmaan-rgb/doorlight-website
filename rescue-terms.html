Rescue Partner Terms

Effective 13 November 2026

These terms apply to every shelter and rescue organisation that lists animals on Doorlight, together with the Terms of Use. Doorlight is currently operated by Maan Hamade, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Contact: doorlightpets@gmail.com.

1. Who can join

Doorlight works with two kinds of organisation, and we verify every one before its listings go live:

  • Government shelters: you give us a named official contact with an official email address, and we confirm the shelter with them. No licence upload is needed.
  • Non-government organisations: you must hold a valid licence or registration from a UAE authority, such as the Ministry of Climate Change and Environment, a municipality, the Community Development Authority or the Department of Economy and Tourism. You upload proof of the licence and give its number, issuing authority and expiry date.

Individuals and unlicensed groups can’t register. Every organisation signs the Code of Conduct below. Verification lasts 12 months, or until your licence expires if that’s sooner. If your licence expires or changes, your listings are paused until we’ve checked the new one. We may suspend an account while we check a concern.

2. Code of Conduct

  • No selling and no breeding.
  • Spay or neuter and vaccinate animals before adoption, where their age allows.
  • Honest listings.
  • Real home checks before an adoption.
  • Take an animal back if an adoption fails.

3. Listings

Adoption only: no price or fee may appear in a listing. List only animals in your care or foster network. Keep details accurate and current, answer every health question truthfully, and disclose known health or behaviour issues. Mark animals as adopted, or remove them, promptly. You confirm you own or may use every photo, and that you follow animal welfare law and the rules of the relevant authorities.

4. Loading your current animals

If you ask us to load your current animals for you, we do so only with your written OK. You confirm each animal’s vaccination, microchip and neutering status before we import it, and you check every listing before your rescue is verified and the listings go live.

5. Adoption decisions

You decide who adopts, using your own adoption agreement, and you handle handover and medical records. Hold meet-and-greets at your premises or another safe place, and give adopters the safety guidance they need.

6. Adopter data

Use adopters’ data only to assess their application and complete the adoption. Keep it confidential, don’t market to adopters without their consent, and delete it when you no longer need it. Tell us within 48 hours of any incident involving data from Doorlight. You’re responsible for how your staff and volunteers handle it.

7. How you behave on Doorlight

Reply to applicants respectfully, and aim to respond within 5 days. Follow the Community and Listing Rules and respond to our moderation requests.

8. Responsibility

You’re responsible for the animals you list and your adoption decisions. If an inaccurate listing, harm caused by an animal in your care, or your misuse of data leads to a claim against us, you agree to cover our reasonable resulting losses, to the extent the law allows. We recommend you hold public liability insurance.

9. Fees

Listing on Doorlight is free. Any paid tools will be optional, announced at least 30 days in advance, and never required in order to list.

10. Suspension and leaving

We may suspend or remove a rescue that breaks these terms or the law. You can leave at any time, and your listings are then removed.

11. Law and changes

These terms follow the same law, disputes and changes clauses as the Terms of Use.
